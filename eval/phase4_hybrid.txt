Q4: What is the internship duration for PG students?
Expected doc:    InternshipGuidelinesUGPG.pdf
Expected answer: More than 6 months, up to one year; no duplicate sources
Retrieval rank:  context 4 / deep 4 (sent to LLM: yes)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The guidelines state that a PG student may undertake an industrial‑training / internship **during the third or fourth semester of the programme** (PG_Manual2024S, page 75).

The earlier internship guidelines (2021) add that for regular M.Tech./M.Plan. students the internship is normally taken in the **III semester**, with the permissible period **from May 15 to December 31**.  If the internship offer is for **more than six months and up to one year**, it may span the III and IV semesters (InternshipGuidelinesUGPG.pdf, page 2).
