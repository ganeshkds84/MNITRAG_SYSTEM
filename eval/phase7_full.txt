- **2024 (most recent)** – The PG Rules & Regulations specify cancellation of the semester examination and an **‘FP’ grade** in every paper of that semester, with all the usual consequences. (PG_Manual2024S.pdf, page 80)  
- **Earlier versions** – The 2022 manual, the 2013 amendment, and the undated UFM document give the same punishment but describe the grade simply as **‘F’** (cancellation of the semester exam and F grade in all papers). Thus, the current (2024) punishment is cancellation of the semester exam and an ‘FP’ grade in all papers.
